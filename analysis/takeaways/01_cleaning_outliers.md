CLEANING
After removing the unusable histories I worked with 62,493 records. Prices rise for years, so a price alone does not show what is unusual, so I added each month's percentage change from the previous month, which the later work uses.
All checks passed, with no zero, negative or missing prices and no duplicates. Price per square foot turned out to be the price divided by one fixed area per history, so it agrees with price perfectly but adds no independent information.
Five areas have under a year of history (Naya Nazimabad Block M, Askari 12, Central Park Block BB, NSIT City and Scheme 33 Sector 34-A), so I left them out of the later statistics and growth rates.

UNUSUAL MONTHS
I flagged any month where a price moved far more than that history normally moves, judged separately for each history so calm and volatile areas are treated fairly. A flagged month also had to move at least 10%, because some histories sit flat for long stretches and made even tiny moves look unusual (my first pass flagged about 11% of months, mostly noise like this).
In the end 403 of 62,493 months (0.6%) were flagged, 3 in citywide histories and the rest in specific areas. Of the 562 histories, 304 had no flagged months and use the average, 253 had some and use the median, and 5 were too short to judge.
The biggest flagged jumps are all in small areas, some rises of 100 to 280% in a single month, like North Karachi Sector 11-C/1 flats in August 2025 (+278%). No real market quadruples in a month, so these are most likely noise from having very few listings that month, not market events. I handle flagged months before any growth calculations.
